Minimal GymMane

A clickable mockup of every screen, redesigned small and flat. It runs offline from this one file, and all workout data in it is sample data.

What we've covered

The app

An Android gym tracker that works 100% offline. You do strength training with cardio, and you want detailed stats, shown visually and without analysis, so you can see where you are lacking and where you are stuck.

Why a new one

Notion, with a table of 8 or 9 columns and many exercises, was too slow. You want its flexible, table-like feel without Notion. The priorities are minimal, fast, and local.

What we looked at

  • GymMane: closest, but too bloated
  • Grit, GymLog: small and offline
  • LibreFit and its fork Nexc, Hypertrack
  • Liftosaur: scriptable, heavier than you need

Decisions so far

  • Keep every element of the workout screen, including Previous, shortened to Prev
  • Much smaller fonts and tighter rows, flat instead of cards
  • A Minimal view switch, with options in Settings for how far it goes
  • The live workout is one table: a single column header and a thin row per exercise
  • Stats for each exercise, charts with a time range you can scroll back through, and an Excel export of the full history

Still open

  • Fork GymMane or build from scratch. A fork must stay GPL-3.0 and credit "Based on GymMane by InlitX", and a switch only hides features, so the code stays.
  • The exact columns of your Notion table. The table in History uses placeholder columns until you list yours.
  • Cardio logging, which this mockup does not include yet.

Your GymMane issue, #363

  • How-to behind a ? icon instead of on every exercise
  • Settings behind an icon at the top
  • Sections collapsed until you tap them
  • Stats per exercise, with time frames you can scroll back through
  • A history page that lists past workouts as text

How to use this page

Switch between Standard and Minimal at the top to compare. Open Settings in the phone to change text size, row height and what the Minimal view hides. Log a set in the workout, press Finish, and the new session shows up in History and Stats.

The Standard look is a rough approximation based on the screenshot you shared. All exercise names, how-to text and numbers are made up.
